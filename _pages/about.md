## Personal Details

Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group. He graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

- **Name:** Junteng Liu
- **Current position:** First-year PhD candidate, HKUST NLP Group
- **Research focus:** Natural language processing and machine learning

## Skills and Research Interests

- Natural Language Processing
- Machine Learning
- LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability

## Academic Background

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, 2024–Present.
  - PhD supervisor: Professor Junxian He.
- **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 2020–2024; graduated June 2024.
  - Previously advised by Professor Junxian He during undergraduate studies at SJTU.
- **Honors and Awards:** Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship, awarded by Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
